Internship · Marjane Group Full-stack platform

Digital Tombola Platform

A platform that generates tombola websites for Marjane's in-store brand campaigns — Monster Energy, Colgate and more. Each campaign gets its own site, branding and domain. Shoppers register with their receipt, which is checked by OCR against the campaign's products and minimum spend, then spin a prize wheel (or enter a prize draw) and get a code to redeem in store.

  • No-code admin back-office: colors, logos, banners, texts, prizes & odds, rules
  • Live prize draws with history, admin accounts and secure sessions
  • Bilingual French / Arabic, mobile-first
